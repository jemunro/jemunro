## Jacob E Munro

**Research Officer | WEHI | Computational Genomics**

I'm a computational genomics researcher at the Walter and Eliza Hall Institute of Medical Research (WEHI). My research focuses on understanding the genetic basis of epilepsy and rare neurological disorders, with an emphasis on identifying disease-causing variants through genome sequencing.

I develop scalable, reproducible bioinformatics tools for analysing genomic data, with applications spanning rare disease diagnosis, structural variation, variant prioritisation and transcriptomics.

### Selected software projects

- **[SVPLEX](https://github.com/bahlolab/SVPLEX)** — A scalable Nextflow pipeline for cohort-level structural variant detection from whole-genome sequencing, integrating multiple callers and read-depth validation.

- **[nf-cavalier](https://github.com/bahlolab/nf-cavalier)** — A Nextflow pipeline for rare disease variant annotation, prioritisation and reporting, integrating phenotype information and family-based analyses.

- **[Edi-Set-Flow](https://github.com/bahlolab/Edi-Set-Flow)** — A workflow for transcriptome-wide RNA editing detection and differential editing analysis using bulk RNA sequencing data.

- **[TBtypeR](https://github.com/bahlolab/TBtypeR)** — An R package for sensitive detection and sublineage classification of mixed *Mycobacterium tuberculosis* infections from whole-genome sequencing data.

- **[PLASTER](https://github.com/bahlolab/PLASTER)** — A pipeline for high-resolution allele typing from PacBio long-read amplicon sequencing, including complex pharmacogenomic loci such as *CYP2D6*.
